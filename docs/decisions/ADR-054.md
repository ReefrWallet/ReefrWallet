# ADR-054: Solana native staking

* **Date:** 2026-11-06
* **Status:** Accepted (implemented 2026-11-06; Trezor screens not yet checked on a device)
* **Context & Problem:** Solana accounts can't stake. ADR-052's Staking tab and ADR-053's stored position are Tron-only. A Solana position is many stake accounts, each with one validator, its own authorities and lockup; rewards compound into the stake, and timing is by epoch.
* **Options Considered:**
  - Reuse Tron's tab with hidden sections: votes, resources and Claim don't apply.
  - Own Solana tab content on the shared Staking Section and sheet parts (chosen).
  - Fresh keypair per stake account: a second signature, which the signing pipeline and Trezor's clear stake screen don't support. Seed-derived accounts (chosen) need one.
  - Whole-account unstake only: simpler, but Trezor Suite offers partial. Partial (chosen) gets generic Trezor screens because it needs a split.
  - Batched transactions for large selections: confirmation counts, partial success and retries. One action, one atomic transaction with a visible selection limit (chosen).
  - Lifetime rewards total: no on-chain counter, so it needs a per-epoch store and backfill, and splits, merges and closed accounts keep it inexact. Last-epoch rewards (chosen).
* **Decision:**
  - **Tab:** Summary (`Staked`, then rewards and APY), Stake Accounts (validator, status, amount), Unstaking (amount, validator, next-epoch time), Ready to Withdraw. Toolbar: Unstake and Stake (`Bar Button Group` `Unstake Stake`), no Claim. Not Staked shows only Stake; watch-only shows no actions.
  - **Restricted rows:** Locked (can unstake, can't withdraw), Can't unstake (staker elsewhere), Can't withdraw (withdrawer elsewhere). They stay visible; sheets disable them and give the reason. Partial unstake is blocked when another wallet holds withdraw authority, since the new account's rent would go to it. Partial on a locked account warns that the new account and rent are locked too.
  - **Notices:** "Some stake accounts may be missing" below Stake Accounts when discovery is degraded; "Couldn't refresh. Showing last known data" below the summary when a read fails. Both are `Section Action` `Prominent` with Retry.
  - **Unresolved transaction:** a section with action, signature, explorer link and Check Again. All staking actions are disabled until it resolves; ordinary sends aren't.
  - **Sheets:** `Solana Transaction Parameters` gains Stake, Select Validator, Unstake, Unstake Partial, Unstake Full Only, Withdraw, Outcome. Unstake and Withdraw are multi-select; a selection over the size limit reads "N of M selected. Repeat for the rest after this confirms." The amount field appears only for one selected account. Outcomes: Submitted, Failed (fee charged), Expired, Unknown, Expired before sending (sign again).
  - **Copy:** timing is approximate and about the next epoch boundary ("Next epoch in about 6 h"), never completion, because warmup and cooldown can span epochs. Rewards read `Last epoch: N SOL`, `Last epoch: at least N SOL`, `Epoch 1076: N SOL` or `Rewards unavailable`, never zero for unknown; with nothing earning, APY only. Withdraw is the app's term; Trezor's screen says "Claim".
  - **Validators:** Stakewiz names, logos, commission and APY, ranked by its score, with `getVoteAccounts` as fallback for custom RPC, non-mainnet or failure.
  - **New Controls:** Stake Account Row, Validator Row, Navigation Bar `Solana Staking`/`Solana Staking Disabled`. **Component variants:** Staking Section `Stake Accounts`, `Stake Accounts Restricted`, `Solana Unstaking`, `Solana Ready to Withdraw`, `Transaction`; Fees `Solana Staking` (read-only).
* **Consequences:** Staking rules live in one chain-specific tab, sharing Tron's section and sheet parts. Validator logos are placeholder initials in Penpot. Sample values are illustrative. Implementation details (discovery, activation port, confirmation and expiry, rewards) get recorded here when shipped.

## Implementation (2026-11-06)

* **WalletKit:** Swift only, no FFI rebuild. `StakeProgram` and `SystemProgram.createAccountWithSeed` build the firmware's exact account lists; new accounts use the seed `stake:N` (the firmware fixture's address is `stake:0`). `SolanaStakeAccount` parses the 200-byte layout.
* **Activation:** an exact port of `stake_activating_and_deactivating_v2` and `warmup_cooldown_allowance.rs` from `solana-program/stake` `cf8925b7cb22403d2e31ad2042deee90b5f3b797`, with upstream's vectors as tests. Rate per epoch from the `reduce_stake_warmup_cooldown` feature account and `getEpochSchedule`. Clock and StakeHistory come from one `getMultipleAccounts`; a failed fetch, or a newest entry other than `epoch − 1`, fails the read, so a lagging node can't reach the reference's "no entry means fully deactivated" branch and offer Withdraw.
* **Discovery:** `getProgramAccounts` on staker and withdrawer replaces the stored list. If either fails, stored addresses are refreshed and `stake:N` seeds scanned, and the read is marked degraded; it drops an account only when shown closed or out of this wallet's authority. If that fails too, the read throws and the stored position stands.
* **Authority:** per action. Deactivate and Split need the staker, and lockup doesn't block them; Withdraw needs the withdrawer and no lockup in force. Custodian bypass isn't supported. Partial is blocked when another wallet withdraws, since the new account's rent would go to it.
* **Transactions:** one action, one atomic transaction, capped by the complete wire size (1232 bytes: 1 + 64 + message with its compute-budget instructions). Simulation sizes the compute limit (plus 20%) and is the last check before signing. Withdrawals take the live balance: a reward paid since the sheet read it would leave less than rent, which the program refuses.
* **Lifecycle:** rebuilt just before signing; `lastValidBlockHeight` travels beside the envelope; past it, nothing is sent and a new signature is requested. A `PendingSubmission` (with `recentBlockhash`) is stored in `StakingDetails` before the broadcast; a failed write stops it. A node rejection means not submitted; any other send failure may have landed, so it's followed and recorded for history under the local signature. Staking stays disabled per account while any submission is unresolved; ordinary sends don't. Reconciliation runs on every position load, apart from the read, and survives relaunch.
* **Resolving:** only `confirmed` or `finalized` resolves a status. `processed` stays pending, since it can vanish with a fork. A failure on-chain reverts staking changes but charges the fee, so the balance refreshes.
* **Proving expiry:** a null status never does, whatever the endpoint. It takes a `finalized` height above validity and a walk of the finalized chain from the block whose hash is `recentBlockhash` through `lastValidBlockHeight`, each step linked by `previousBlockhash` and height + 1, with the signature in none. The cursor is stored, so the walk resumes after 429s and relaunches; public RPC rate-limits `getBlock` hard, so expect minutes. Any gap or error keeps the outcome unknown.
* **Rewards:** eligibility is never inferred from looking active (split children inherit an activation epoch; closed accounts vanish). `null` counts as not earning only if never delegated, or if the ported calculation at the reward epoch gives zero from real history entries with the delegation already in place. Addresses that leave stay queryable by the epoch their disappearance was **detected**, until the target epoch passes it. Fully resolved answers are cached per epoch and address set; unknowns retry hourly.
* **Reward payout window:** the Stake program refuses changes (`EpochRewardsActive`, `0x10`) while paying an epoch's rewards. Simulation catches it and the sheet says to try again in a few minutes. Found on localnet, not in the plan.
* **Timing:** next-boundary only, from the Clock slot, the epoch schedule and `getRecentPerformanceSamples`, else elapsed time since `epochStartTimestamp`.
* **Stakewiz** is its own rate-gate provider. Validators are cached like Tron's representatives.
* **Limits:** rewards cover addresses this wallet has seen; an account opened and closed elsewhere unseen isn't counted. An endpoint that can't serve the blocks of the walk (a pruned custom RPC) keeps staking blocked on that account, with no override. The endpoint is the trust root for what it returns. Staked SOL stays out of account totals, as Tron's does.
* **Coverage:** unit tests, localnet (lifecycle, separate authorities, lockup, expiry walk), a mainnet read against a real staker, and one Mac UI test (stake, partial unstake, withdraw). Not run: a Trezor device; the app on an iPhone beyond SwiftUI previews.
