# ADR-059: Cardano native staking

* **Date:** 2026-11-08
* **Status:** Accepted (implemented 2026-11-08; Trezor screens and Preprod runs not yet checked, see Implementation)
* **Context & Problem:** Cardano accounts can't stake. They send ADA and sign certificates and withdrawals only for CIP-30 dapps, and ADR-013, 014 and 058 list staking as out of scope. ADR-052/053/054's Staking tab covers Tron and Solana. Delegation doesn't lock ADA, but the stake address's total can include UTxOs that another payment key holds under the same stake key (ADR-058). Rewards sit in a reward account and leave only through a full withdrawal; deregistration needs a zero reward balance; withdrawals need the stake key delegated to a DRep since Plomin; Trezor rejects combined certificates.
* **Options Considered:**
  - Count delegated stake as staked ADA in totals: wrong, it's spendable and may include other keys' UTxOs. Display-only (chosen); only withdrawable rewards count toward account value.
  - Spend any of the stake address's UTxOs: not signable. Coin selection and affordability use signable UTxOs only (chosen).
  - One combined registration+delegation certificate: Trezor rejects it. Separate certificates, always (chosen).
  - Probe on Preprod whether a missing DRep is checked before or after the withdrawal: needs rewards to accrue. A missing DRep is always two steps (chosen), correct whichever way the ledger orders the checks.
  - Delegate pool and vote in one transaction: fewer steps, but each change is independent and a vote-only change must exist anyway. Independent actions (chosen).
  - Pools from several indexers: Blockfrost only (chosen).
* **Decision:**
  - **Tab:** Summary (`Delegated:`, next epoch time, rewards-start note after a first delegation), Delegation (delegated stake, pool, DRep; Change Pool, Change DRep), Rewards (available, total earned, `Epoch N` latest reward; Withdraw), a Continue section while a two-step action is stored, and ADR-054's Unresolved Transaction and notices. Toolbar: Stop Staking and Stake, each disabled when it doesn't apply or while anything is in flight (`Bar Button Group` `Cardano Staked`, `Cardano Not Staked`, `Cardano Pending`). Watch-only shows no actions; Trezor on iPhone is refused before any prompt.
  - **Sheets:** `Cardano Transaction Parameters` gains Stake (pool required, DRep optional with Skip), Select Pool, Select DRep, Change Pool, Change DRep, Withdraw Rewards and Stop Staking (each with Step 1 and Step 2) and Outcome. Fees `Cardano Staking` shows the deposit or the refund. New controls: Pool Row, DRep Row.
  - **DRep:** Always Abstain, No Confidence or a pasted id (CIP-129 and CIP-105 bech32, checked on Blockfrost and shown by name).
  - **Two steps:** with no DRep, Withdraw (and Stop Staking with rewards) first delegates a vote, then acts. `CardanoStakingWorkflow` (intent, DRep, phase, time) lives in `CardanoStakingDetails`, apart from pending submissions, and is this device's own record: written before step 1 is broadcast, never overwritten by reads. Phases: `needsVoteDelegation(notice)`, `voteDelegationPending(hash)`, `readyForFinalStep(notice)`, `finalStepPending(hash)`. Step 1 resolved and the live read showing the DRep promotes to `readyForFinalStep`; an expired or rejected step returns to its start with a notice; step 2 confirming clears it. Cancel clears only the workflow: a pending submission stays stored, keeps blocking, and only refreshes when it resolves.
  - **Reads:** Blockfrost `accounts/{stake}` (`registered`, not `active`, which is delegation; a 404 is an unregistered, never-used address), `registrations` (the refund is the latest registration's deposit; unreadable blocks Stop Staking, no `key_deposit` fallback), `rewards?count=1` (stored with its epoch), `epochs/latest`, `pools/extended` paged and filtered by `pools/retiring` and `pools/retired` (cached 24 h per endpoint), `pools/{id}` (+ `/updates`, `/metadata`) and `governance/dreps/{id}` (+ `/metadata`) to revalidate what the picker cached.
  - **Building:** `cardano_build_staking` (CSL 17 `CertificatesBuilder`, `WithdrawalsBuilder`) over signable UTxOs with `LargestFirstMultiAsset`, assets kept in change. Conway `reg_cert`/`unreg_cert` with explicit deposit and refund; implicit inputs and deposits balance in CSL. Signing paths add `2/0`. Change that can't make a valid output is never burned as fee: CSL's burning is off and coin selection adds inputs instead. Funding is validated only by the build: a shortfall (or a change output below min-ADA) comes back as a typed value, not an error string.
  - **Signing:** local through `cardano_sign_transaction`. Trezor through the dapp path (`cardano_sign_dapp_tx`, which maps certificates and withdrawals and checks the body hash); the returned witness set is attached to the canonical body.
  - **Lifecycle:** the TTL is the tip slot plus 2 h. `submit` re-checks it against a fresh tip and sends nothing with under 10 minutes left; the submission is stored before the broadcast (a failed write stops it); a Blockfrost ledger rejection means not submitted, any other error means pending. Resolved once `txs/{hash}` exists; expired once a block 15 deep has a slot above the TTL and the transaction still isn't seen. After a confirmation the position and balances refresh, from the sheet's follow and from reconciliation alike.
  - **History labels:** the CBOR of every stake-account transaction is decoded (`cardano_decode_transaction`), counting only certificates and withdrawals of the account's own stake credential (the decoder's new `subject`, which unlike `credential` also names a legacy registration's key): Stake, Delegate, Vote Delegation, Withdraw Rewards, Stop Staking, combined with ` + `. Blockfrost's counts aren't used: they may omit vote delegations, and net-delta heuristics fail because another payment key may pay the fee.
  - **Storage:** `StakingDetails.cardano(CardanoStakingDetails)`; `staked` is delegated stake (display only) and `rewards` the withdrawable amount; Cardano's account value counts rewards only.
* **Consequences:** Cardano staking works for local and Trezor (macOS) accounts, read-only for watch-only. A withdrawal with no DRep costs two transactions and fees. Stale pool lists are caught by revalidation. History costs one extra Blockfrost call per transaction, once, since history is persisted. Out of scope: the dapp certificate preview shows no pool or DRep target; a `stake1…` address may pass as a send recipient; the Preview testnet; a one-transaction DRep + withdrawal.

## Implementation (2026-11-08)

* **WalletKit:** `cardano_build_staking`, `cardano_unsigned_transaction` and `cardano_assemble_with_witness_set` in Rust; `CardanoClient+Staking` and `CardanoStaking` in Swift. DRep ids go through `DRep::from_bech32`, confirmed to accept both CIP-105 (28-byte) and CIP-129 (29-byte) forms.
* **App:** `CardanoStakingPositionLoader` (behind `CardanoStakingSource`), `CardanoStakingService`, `CardanoStakingModel`, view, sheet and pickers on the shared `StakingSection` and `TransactionSheet`; `StakingSection` gained a secondary action and per-action disabling.
* **Verified:** Rust and Swift unit tests (every action, deposit and refund balancing, a withdrawal or refund funding the fee, shortfall, preserved native assets, separate certificates, revalidation, TTL re-check, refresh after confirmation, every workflow transition including relaunch and Cancel with a pending step), macOS and iOS builds, previews of the tab and sheets.
* **Live Preprod (manual, not in CI, which budgets under a minute per test):** stake with a DRep, change pool, vote-only DRep change and stop staking were built, signed and accepted by the ledger; the 2 ADA deposit was paid and refunded, and registration, delegation and vote delegation went as separate certificates. It found two bugs, fixed with unit tests: Blockfrost returns the submitted hash quoted, and a retry of a landed transaction is rejected as "All inputs are spent… probably already been included", which Conway also says when a different transaction spent them. That message counts as success if `txs/{hash}` exists (wallet and dapp submissions alike), and as a rejection only once an input's `consumed_by_tx` names a different transaction. Until the index shows either, the outcome is unresolved and the submission stays tracked, with the outputs it spends stored beside its hash so every reconciliation rechecks for a competing spend (one found ends it as expired), as does a failed lookup: an earlier attempt the client never saw answered may still confirm. Blockfrost's account view lags `txs/{hash}` by a few blocks, so a build right after a confirmation can spend stale UTxOs and be rejected; the person rebuilds. History labels from the vote-only transaction were not read back.
* **Not verified:** a Trezor device (registration and delegation, vote delegation, withdrawal and deregistration screens); a withdrawal with real rewards and the two-step flow on chain; the iPhone Account Tabs still show the Solana chip set (no Addresses chip).
* **Deviation from plan:** history decodes the CBOR for every stake-account transaction rather than only when Blockfrost's counts are non-zero, until a Preprod check shows whether the counts include vote delegations.
