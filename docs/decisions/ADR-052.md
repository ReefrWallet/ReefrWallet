# ADR-052: TRX staking design

* **Date:** 2026-11-05
* **Status:** Accepted (implemented 2026-11-05; Solana has its own tab, ADR-054; Trezor vote/claim need `make walletkit-ffi`)
* **Context & Problem:** Tron accounts can't stake, vote or claim rewards. Trezor Suite uses full-screen staking pages; this app presents every transaction as a sheet (ADR-042).
* **Options Considered:**
  - Trezor-style pages with a Freeze → Vote stepper: familiar, but a second transaction layout.
  - Staking tab plus transaction sheets (chosen): one sheet layout for sends and staking. Stake and Vote stay separate signatures, as they are on-chain.
* **Decision:**
  - **Tab:** Staking in account details, Tron here (Solana: ADR-054). Mac: Segmented Control `Staking`. iPhone: Account Tabs `Staking`, no filter toolbar. Sections: Staking Summary (Staked with no USD, then Rewards with USD and APR; Not Staked shows Staked 0 TRX, with the note as a footnote below the last section), Votes, Resources, Unstaking. States: Staked, Votes Unassigned, Not Staked.
  - **Density:** one-line rows, no column headers, titles only in section headers. Addresses, per-representative APR, per-resource stake and unlock dates live in the sheets. Sections stack in one column on both platforms: Votes, Resources, Unstaking, Ready to Withdraw. Each is a title above a rounded group of rows, like dApp settings (Staking Section component). Section actions (Vote, Cancel All, Withdraw) sit below their rows, right-aligned. Unassigned votes show a note at the leading edge of Vote's row (Section Action `Prominent`).
  - **Actions:** Send shows only on the Token Balances tab. On the Staking tab the toolbar has Claim, Unstake and Stake as one grouped pill (`Bar Button Group`, a `ControlGroup`) with icons, or icons and text per the toolbar's display mode (Navigation Bar `Staking`); Not Staked shows only Stake (`Not Staked`). Icons `lockOpen` and `arrowDownCircle` are magenta placeholders.
  - **Sheets:** `Tron Transaction Parameters` gains Usecases Stake, Unstake, Vote, Select Representatives, Claim Rewards, Withdraw, Cancel Unstaking. All but the picker stack parameters → Fees → Estimate Fees, Cancel, Next. Stake and Unstake show the balance under From; Unstake also shows Resource reduction (delta, old → new) after Amount. Vote opens after a Stake and from the tab; "Select Representatives…" opens the picker.
  - **Fees:** new `Fees` Blockchain `Tron Staking`, read-only. System contracts spend Bandwidth, with no TRC-20 fee limit to edit.
  - **Withdraw** acts on one Ready to Withdraw section, since `withdrawExpireUnfreeze` releases all expired amounts. **Cancel All** matches `cancelAllUnfreezeV2`, which has no per-entry form.
  - **New Controls:** Progress Bar, Amount Stat, Resource Row, Representative Row, Unstaking Row, Content Section Header, Section Action. **New Components:** Staking Summary, Staking Section, Sheet Buttons.
  - **Protocol rules shown in copy, from memory, to confirm against Tron docs when implementing:** 1 TRX = 1 vote, 14-day unstake wait, up to 9 representatives per vote (Trezor's per-transaction limit, applied to all keys), votes reduced proportionally on unstake, rewards claimable every 24 hours. Tronscan's page was behind a bot check and was not reviewed.
* **Consequences:** Staking inherits the send sheet's behavior. Each action costs one hardware confirmation, so stake-then-vote takes two. With every section showing, the tab scrolls (frames: Mac 800 pt, iPhone 999 pt). Sample Bandwidth costs other than Vote (271) and Claim (247) are placeholders.

## Implementation (2026-11-05)

* **WalletKit:** `TronRawTransaction` decodes `VoteWitnessContract` (4), `WithdrawBalanceContract` (13) and `CancelAllUnfreezeV2Contract` (59), fail-closed (repeated fields only for votes; the deprecated `support` is rejected). `TronClient+Staking` reads the position (`getaccount`, `getaccountresource`, `getReward`, `getcanwithdrawunfreezeamount`) and asks the node to build each contract (`freezebalancev2`, `unfreezebalancev2`, `withdrawexpireunfreeze`, `cancelallunfreezev2`, `votewitnessaccount`, `withdrawbalance`); the returned `raw_data_hex` must decode to exactly the requested contract. Representatives come from TronScan `api/vote/witness` (name, `annualizedRate`), falling back to the node's `listwitnesses` (no names or rates).
* **Rust:** `TronContract::VoteWitness`/`WithdrawBalance` flow through walletkit-hardware, walletkit-trezor (`TronVoteWitnessContract`, `TronWithdrawBalance`) and walletkit-ffi (`to_tron_tx_fields`, `tron_raw_data`, vectors from TronWeb 6.5.1). More than 9 votes is refused for Trezor. Cancel-all has no Trezor firmware contract: it is software-key only, and `TronStakingService` refuses it (and any transaction that isn't `isTrezorReproducible`) for hardware accounts before a prompt. Until `make walletkit-ffi` rebuilds the xcframework, vote and claim fail that check on Trezor, and the two matching `signingEnvelopeRebuildsTheSameRawData` cases fail.
* **App:** `TronStakingService` validates actions against the live position, has the node build them, and signs and broadcasts through `TransactionService`, then waits up to about 30 s for execution. State was first read live; since ADR-053 it is stored per account and the tab renders the stored position. `AccountTab.staking` (Tron only) shows `AccountStakingView`; `TronStakingSheet` is one `TransactionSheet` for every action, with the shared Tron section of `TransactionFeesView`, and `TronRepresentativePicker`. Dapp requests for the new contracts are previewed by `TronTransactionParametersView`.
* **Limits:** one vote per TRX staked, counting stake delegated to others (the node's `tronPowerLimit`, else `frozenV2` plus delegated balances, so Vote stays available when everything is delegated); Claim stays available while rewards remain after a full unstake; at most 9 representatives; 32 pending unstakes (java-tron); minimum stake 1 TRX; Bandwidth and Energy only (no `TRON_POWER`).
* **Tron fees (all transactions):** transfers, dapp requests and staking show one Tron Fees section: the optional TRC-20 fee limit, Bandwidth (and Energy for contract calls) used with whether the account's available resources, staked or free, cover it ("Covered by your resources (810 available)" or what burning the shortfall costs), then the USD cost of the burn. `TronFeeEstimate` carries the available resources and the bandwidth/energy burn; `Fees.tronFee` conveys it. Transfers estimate from the transaction the node builds, so a send fully covered by resources reserves nothing from MAX. Not covered: the extra TRX burned when a transfer activates an unused account. The Penpot `Fees` Tron variants still show the old rows.
