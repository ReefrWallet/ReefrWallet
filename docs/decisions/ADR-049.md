# ADR-049: Choosing and remembering a dapp's account per chain

* **Date:** 2026-10-27
* **Status:** Accepted
* **Context & Problem:** The browser has one selected account. A dapp that asked to connect on another chain (Minswap/Cardano while an EVM account was selected) was refused with a generic error. The sidebar's dapp pane showed raw account addresses, couldn't change them, and listed no chains a dapp hadn't been approved on.
* **Options Considered:**
  - Auto-switch to a remembered or first account: no prompt, but the user never picks.
  - Ask which account on a chain mismatch: one extra prompt, only when needed.
  - Keep refusing: simplest, leaves the error.
* **Decision:** Ask, and remember.
  - Providers declare their connect requests (`DappProvider.isConnectRequest`; quiet `onlyIfTrusted` checks are not). Before such a request reaches its provider, `DappBridge` records the namespace. A chain the dapp is already connected on with the selected account needs nothing; if no account on it is selected but one is remembered, the bridge switches to it silently.
  - Otherwise the request joins the other connect requests the page makes within 300 ms, and `DappAccountChoiceView` shows one prompt with an account drop-down per chain ("key store · account (address)"), the selected or remembered account preselected, even when an account of that chain is already selected. Approving grants each chain's permission and selects the account, so the providers' own connect prompts are skipped. A request that arrives after the prompt opened starts a new prompt.
  - Grants already hold the remembered account per chain; opening a dapp now selects the account it was last connected with.
  - `ConnectedDappModel.serializedNamespaces` (default `[]`, a lightweight migration) holds the chains the page asked to connect on, saved on the first request even before approval; requests before the dapp has an entry are kept in memory until it does.
  - The dapp's detail pane shows one account drop-down per such chain, limited to accounts on that chain, with "Not connected".
* **Consequences:** A chain mismatch becomes one prompt instead of an error. A dapp that connects on several chains at once gets one prompt. The toolbar shows one active account, but every chain the dapp holds a grant on stays served on its connected account (`DappSession.selectedAccount`), so Ethereum and Solana both work while one is on screen; before, only the active chain did, and the last one connected won. A chain a dapp merely probes with a connect call appears in the pane even if never approved. Cancelling the chooser rejects the request with the chain's refused error.
