# ADR-013: Blockfrost-backed Cardano foundation

* **Date:** 2026-09-21
* **Status:** Accepted
* **Context & Problem:** Cardano needs CIP-1852 Icarus Ed25519-BIP32 keys and account-level UTxO identity, neither of which is compatible with WalletKit's SLIP-0010 Ed25519 implementation.
* **Decision:** Use a Cardano ledger FFI for CIP-1852 account xpub and Shelley base-address derivation. Persist BIP-39 entropy plus wordlist as every local keystore's sole secret representation, deriving the conventional seed only in memory. Target Blockfrost mainnet and Preprod with build-environment defaults and Settings overrides.
* **Consequences:** Seed-only local keystores are unsupported and cannot be recovered after migration. Cardano watch-only import is account-xpub based. Local accounts can construct, sign, and submit native ADA transfers from pure-ADA UTxOs at external address `0/0`; asset-bearing UTxOs and native-asset sends remain read-only until multi-asset change and address discovery are implemented. Staking, governance, hardware signing, and arbitrary-magic networks remain separate work.
